The Roof Yard Story

An Oasis Suspended Above The City

Roof Yard was born from a singular passion: to create an enchanting rooftop escape where serene greenery, warm fairy lights, and culinary sophistication converge.

Whether you are sipping cold-pressed botanicals during the golden hour sunset or savoring prime Australian ribeyes beneath starlit night skies, every single moment at Roof Yard is meticulously curated for romance, celebration, and pure serenity.
